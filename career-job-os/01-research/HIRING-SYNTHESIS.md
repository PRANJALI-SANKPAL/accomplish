# Research synthesis — hiring & shortlisting (2026)

Sources synthesized: HireStepX India 2026 strategy, OwnYourCareer FAANG/Microsoft/Adobe/JPMC guides, Instahyre DSA roadmap, recruiter LinkedIn/resume mismatch guides, company career pages (Nutanix/Akamai patterns), Wells Fargo Workday notes, prior deep audit of Pranjali’s multi-company resumes.

## Shared funnel (all large employers)

```text
Apply/Referral → Hard filters → ATS rank → Recruiter 15–30s (+LinkedIn) → HM skim → OA/Phone → Loop → Offer → BGV
```

Most of Pranjali’s rejections happen **before OA**: Gate 2–3 (rank + trust).

## Conversion reality (directional)

| Channel | Interview rate (directional) |
|---|---|
| Cold portal | 1–5% |
| Strong referral + fit | often 10–20× cold |
| Interview → offer | 15–25% if prep ready |

## Why AI-tailored resumes fail in 2025–26

Recruiters flooded with JD-mirrored AI resumes. Standard defense: open LinkedIn. Mismatch on title/dates/stack/claims → silent reject. Tailoring must be emphasis-only.

## Company-type physics

### Product Big Tech (Amazon, Microsoft, Adobe)
- Level mapping is ruthless (L4 vs L5 / SDE1 vs SDE2)
- Need ownership + metrics + coding bar readiness
- Referral helps visibility, not content quality
- Amazon: Leadership Principles language in bullets helps humans
- Microsoft: cloud/backend relevance to Job ID; OA follows screen
- Adobe: Workday + product-lane fit (Experience/Document/Creative/Firefly)

### Edge / infra (Akamai, Nutanix)
- Domain depth > app CRUD
- Akamai: networking/Linux/systems; resume claims get deep-dived
- Nutanix: often mid/senior systems (OS/storage); many reqs 8–12+ YOE
- Wrong physics for pure Spring Boot consulting unless role is clearly app backend

### Bank / payments GCC (JPMC, Wells Fargo, Mastercard, PayPal)
- Workday/Oracle ATS + high volume
- Consistency and regulated-env authenticity matter
- Keyword match important but exaggeration is toxic
- Repeat applications with shifting stories hurt (Wells 6–7×)

### Product fintech / startups (GROWW, similar)
- Ownership and shipping speed
- Less degree snobbery
- Still rejects fake architecture narratives

## Resume standards that pass humans

- 1 page for <8 YOE
- Single column, standard headings
- XYZ bullets: Action + metric + method
- ≥60% bullets with numbers
- Skills only if interviewable
- LinkedIn/GitHub URLs consistent
- Same facts everywhere

## Job search OS that works (India)

Daily: targeted applies + referral asks + DSA  
Weekly: tracker review, Naukri refresh, follow-ups at ~10 business days  
Prep: pattern DSA 150–250, LLD for 2–3 YOE, light HLD, STAR stories  
Timeline upward move: commonly 8–20 weeks
