# Playbook — Product Fintech / Startups (GROWW and similar)

## What they optimize for
- Shipping ownership, speed, product sense, APIs that users touch
- Less pedigree snobbery than FAANG

## Resume emphasis
- End-to-end ownership language **only if true**
- Fintech transaction/analytics systems
- Fast iteration, Docker/AWS, measurable outcomes

## Process
- Often referral/LinkedIn heavy
- May include assignment
- Interviews probe “what did YOU own?”

## Do not
- Architecture theater
- Fake “6+ microservices owned” unless defensible in detail
